Rise of AI Companies

How AI firms moved from niche innovators to the primary drivers of modern equity growth.

Milestones in AI Market Expansion

Cloud infrastructure, GPU breakthroughs, and generative AI releases transformed investor expectations. Capital shifted heavily toward firms with AI-ready products and data moats.

Key names include NVIDIA, Microsoft, Alphabet, Meta, and AMD.
