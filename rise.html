Data Methodology & Mathematical Normalization

Empirical Data Origins

Historical pricing structures reflect split-adjusted closing values from 2018 through 2025. Broad market movements track the S&P 500 Index (^GSPC), while the AI Megacap Basket aggregates a custom capital-weighted portfolio of market leaders: NVIDIA (NVDA), Microsoft (MSFT), Alphabet (GOOGL), Meta (META), and Amazon (AMZN). Baseline points were pulled via the Yahoo Finance API and cross-verified using Bloomberg Terminal datasets.

The Indexing Formula

To isolate and compare relative growth rates rather than raw share prices, all values are normalized to a base level of 100 on January 1, 2018. The year-over-year values are calculated as follows:

Indexed Valuet = ( Pricet / Price2018 ) × 100
