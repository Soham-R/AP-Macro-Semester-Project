The Research Question

Does AI improve markets - or threaten them?

Before AI, financial trading was driven entirely by human decisions. Today, algorithms and machine learning account for the majority of trades executed globally. This project investigates whether that shift is a net positive for economic stability, or whether it introduces dangerous new risks that regulators have yet to address.

Thesis: AI improves market efficiency but poses increased systemic risks without proper regulation.

Market Efficiency

AI reduces transaction costs and increases liquidity across global markets.

Volatility Risk

Algorithmic feedback loops can accelerate crashes and market instability.

Global Contagion

AI-driven shocks in one market can instantly ripple worldwide.

Regulation Gap

Policy frameworks have not kept pace with the speed of AI adoption in finance.
