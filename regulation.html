Section 05

Regulation

Policy options to preserve innovation while reducing systemic market risk.

Current Policy Landscape

Regulators have focused on market abuse, operational resilience, and model governance, but fragmented standards make cross-border oversight difficult.

AI-specific finance rules are still emerging compared with the pace of private-sector deployment.

SEC guidanceESMA risk frameworksBasel model controlsNational AI acts

What Effective Oversight Could Include

Model Audit Trails

Require traceable logs for training data, parameter updates, and live model decisions.

Stress Testing

Run scenario tests on AI trading systems for liquidity shocks and correlated failures.

Dynamic Circuit Breakers

Use adaptive safeguards that account for model speed and cross-market feedback.

Regulatory Thesis

Well-designed regulation should not block AI adoption. It should reduce tail risk, improve transparency, and maintain fair market access as trading systems become more autonomous.
