Section 04

Risks & Volatility

Where AI-driven speed and concentration can create fragility in financial systems.

Core Risk Channels

AI strategies can crowd into similar signals, increasing correlation across institutions. During stress, synchronized selling or deleveraging can amplify drawdowns and reduce market depth.

  • Algorithmic feedback loops and momentum cascades.
  • Concentration risk in a small number of AI megacaps.
  • Cross-border contagion through real-time global linkages.

Illustrative Volatility Distribution

A stylized comparison suggests AI-dominant trading environments may reduce calm periods but increase severe tail events.

Systemic Concern

When market infrastructure is heavily automated, small data errors or model mis-specification can propagate rapidly. Monitoring, circuit breakers, and explainability standards become essential safeguards.
